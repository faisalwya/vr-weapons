Weapons Sandbox VR

This screen is a self-playing preview. Open this link in the Quest browser to play it yourself.

Sword
Reach over your right shoulder and squeeze grip to draw. Swing hard to cut; throw it and it can stick.
Pistol
Right hip. Trigger fires. A or X drops the magazine; take a fresh one from your left hip and push it up into the grip.
Put away
Let go at the holster. Anywhere else, it drops or flies; it finds its way home.
Recall
Empty hand: hold grip and pull the trigger.
Move
Left stick forward to aim a jump, release to land. Right stick turns.
